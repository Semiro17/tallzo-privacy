Delete Your Tallzo Account

We're sorry to see you go! You can delete your Tallzo account and all associated data using either method below:

Method 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open the Tallzo app
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm deletion

Method 2: Email Request

Send an email to hello@tallzo.app with:

  • Subject: "Delete My Account"
  • Include: Your registered email address

We'll process your request within 48 hours.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile information (name, email)
  • All habit tracking data (sleep, food, exercise records)
  • Height measurements and progress data
  • Uploaded food photos
  • Account credentials and authentication data

Data Retention

All personal data is deleted immediately and permanently. We do not retain any personal information after account deletion.

Exception: Subscription transaction records are retained for 7 years as required by tax law, but these contain no personal identifying information beyond what's legally required.
